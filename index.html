The Five Core Classes

GAMEPLAY

What make us Unique

Our gameplay is built to give you the most unique TCG experience. Its simple enough for anyone to understand, yet deep enough to stay competitive.

Rank Up System

In Grandis Legacy there is no instant, effortless evolutions. You have to tribute your Skill Cards as EXP to rank up your heroes.

Three Heroes Party

You control 3 Heroes instead of just one. Strategize your formation, combine their synergies, and defeat all 3 enemy heroes to win the games.

Legacy Card

When your hero falls, their Legacy stayed with Legacy card that empowers your remaining heroes.

No Summon Unit

Unlike any other TCG that always summoning units. You only control 3 heroes that can use Skill Cards to defeat your opponent.
